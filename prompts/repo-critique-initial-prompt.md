# Repo Critique // Initial Prompt

Use this prompt to invoke an open-ended structural critique of a repo against its grounding note. This is one of two prompts that operationalize a fresh-context critique cycle: pair with `repo-critique-synthesis-prompt.md` to fold independent critiques into a plan.

The cycle is warranted at thresholds where the project's structure, posture, or direction needs re-examination — not as a standing review.

---

Review the current state of the repo and surface high-level observations — concerns, doubts, or things that don't make sense — anchored in the grounding note and the higher-level purpose articulated therein.

Rely on durable truth:

- the repo as it currently is (read it directly)
- the grounding note maintained outside the repo

Do not rely on:

- thread history or accumulated explanations from prior sessions
- operator summaries or narrative framing of the project's state

The point is context reset: reconstruct the project from durable truth and surface what reads unclear, contradictory, overclaimed, stale, or structurally awkward. Open-ended is the posture; the durable backbone does the focusing.

This is a critique, not a plan. Produce observations, not corrective actions.
